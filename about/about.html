THE IDEA

Make the clothes you own easier to style.

FitFind is a shopping-assistant concept that starts with what you already have. Instead of searching for an entire outfit, upload one piece and discover complementary items, visual inspiration and the best shopping options.

For the first version

Visual-first

Simple image upload and inspiration-led browsing.

India-first shopping

Focused on familiar platforms such as Myntra, Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho and Nykaa Fashion.

UI before AI

This prototype focuses only on the product experience and interface.
